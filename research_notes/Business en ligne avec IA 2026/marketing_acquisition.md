# Marketing & client-acquisition service businesses for a non-technical solo entrepreneur in France (2026, amplified by AI)

Research date: 22 October 2026. Reader profile: a French founder who already runs a small remote web-design agency for associations (loi 1901), with little technical skill.

Source-quality warning (applies throughout): most French pricing data online in 2026 comes from agency or vendor blogs (SODIGIX, Boustacom, Sunleads, CalculateurBTP, habitat86, GET-GEO, Linker, etc.). These are self-interested and often SEO or AI-written pages. Treat their figures as order-of-magnitude market ranges, not audited data. Primary sources (Légifrance, DGCCRF, CNIL, gouv.fr, Malt barometer, WSJ, Google) are flagged where used. No income claim found anywhere was independently verified.

---

## 1. Media buying / paid ads management (Meta, Google, TikTok) for local businesses and e-commerce

### Takeaway
Ads management is still sold everywhere in France, at €390–€1,500+/month fixed fees or 8–20% of ad spend (often with a minimum fee). But Meta's and Google's automation (Advantage+, Andromeda, Performance Max, Meta's stated goal of fully automated ads by end of 2026) has turned "button-pushing" into a commodity. What clients still pay for is creative strategy (many distinct ad concepts), tracking/offer/landing-page work and accountability. It is crowded ("SMMA" has been saturated since around 2017), churn-prone, and needs real skill in tracking and creative testing. That makes it a weak first move for a non-technical solo founder, unless it is bundled with landing pages they already build.

### Cited Findings
**What it is / pricing models (France)**
- Two dominant models: a fixed monthly fee or a % of ad spend. The % "generally between 8 and 20%" is degressive with budget, and fixed fees run "between €500 and €5,000 per month" (2024 article, older) — [deux.io](https://deux.io/tarif-agence-google-ads/)
- Example public grid: SODIGIX Google Ads management starts at €390 HT/month excluding media budget. For media budgets above €10k/month it charges 10% of spend with a €1,290 HT minimum (May 2026) — [SODIGIX](https://sodigix.com/blog/tarif-agence-google-ads/)
- A Strasbourg SEA freelancer charges 10–15% of the managed budget and €400/day for one-off audits or training. They claim agencies charge "20–30%" (self-interested comparison) — [Trust SEO](https://trust-seo.fr/services/consultant-sea-strasbourg)
- Freelancers position themselves for clients spending at least about €1,500/month on ads — [adwords-consultant.com](https://adwords-consultant.com/)
- Malt day rates for Google/Meta ads lead-gen freelancers seen in listings: roughly €290–€650/day (e.g., €290, €350, €400, €450, €485, €550, €600, €650) — [Malt lead-gen tag](https://www.malt.fr/s/tags/generation-de-leads-59ca868b6e6c295df40aa40b); [Malt EN](https://en.malt.fr/s/tags/lead-generation-59ca868b6e6c295df40aa40b?p=6)
- Malt 2026 barometer: experienced "webmarketing" consultants average €523/day and SEO consultants €498/day. Average day rates are €309 at 0–2 years of experience and €393 at 3–7 years — [Malt barometer marketing](https://www.malt.fr/t/barometre-tarifs/marketing)

**How AI changed it**
- WSJ (June 2025): Meta aims to let brands fully create and target ads with AI by end of 2026, from a product image and a budget goal. Zuckerberg described a future where a business gives its objective, price per result and bank account "and then we just do the rest" — [Yahoo/Verdict summarising WSJ](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/meta-looking-fully-automate-ad-113056107.html); [WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/meta-aims-to-fully-automate-ad-creation-using-ai-7d82e249)
- Meta internal study: its AI ad tools drove a 22% improvement in ROAS (Meta's own claim, not independent) — [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/406315/meta-plans-to-automate-all-ad-creation-by-2026-re.html?edition)
- April 2026: Meta's Andromeda retrieval system (rolling out since late 2024) changed delivery. Practitioners say it rewards "several fully distinct concepts and stories" rather than micro-variations, so advertisers must produce far more creative. Meta pushes broad audiences and fewer ad sets, and Manus (an AI agent Meta acquired in Dec 2025) is appearing in Ads Manager. Big brands remain wary of AI-generated creative — [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2026/04/07/meta-ai-ad-creation)
- Meta says that if manual controls go beyond thresholds, campaigns switch to "Advantage+ off" and the system prompts re-enabling automation — [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2026/04/07/meta-ai-ad-creation)
- Digiday: Meta's all-in-one AI tools are "geared to smaller businesses that may not have been able to afford an agency partner anyway". In other words, Meta targets exactly the local-SMB segment beginners sell to — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/metas-ai-ad-plan-raises-stakes-even-if-creative-execs-are-shrugging-it-off/)

**Saturation / economics (practitioner sources, biased)**
- A vendor comparison that is trying to sell an alternative model describes SMMA (social media marketing agency) as "Extreme (saturated since 2017)". It says local markets have "10–50+ agencies competing" and margins are 20–40%. It is self-interested (sells AI receptionist white-label) — [VoiceAI Connect](https://www.myvoiceaiconnect.com/blog/ai-receptionist-agency-vs-smma)
- Reddit synthesis (also by a vendor): SMMA margins are about 30–50%, and "SMMA clients judge you every single month". Churn is "where SMMA quietly loses people" — [Ciela AI](https://ciela.ai/blogs/smma-vs-ai-automation-agency-reddit)
- Focus Digital 2026 report: "60% of senior marketing leaders reduced agency spend due to AI", with "soft churn" where retainers shrink 20–30% without cancellation. Delivery dissatisfaction is cited by 48% of departing clients. US agency sample, methodology not detailed — [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-marketing-agency-churn/)

### Inferences
- Realistic entry for this founder: "landing page + Meta/Google lead campaign" packages for the kinds of clients they already serve (associations' fundraising, events, local SMBs), priced around €300–€600/month plus setup. Pure ads management at 10–15% of spend only works once clients spend €2k+/month, which few associations or artisans do.
- AI lowers the bar for setting up campaigns but raises it for creative volume and strategy. Value has moved from managing accounts to making creative and offers, where AI image and video tools help a non-technical person.
- Results are hard to guarantee and are judged monthly, so expect high churn. Promising ROAS to beginners' clients is the main reputational risk.

### Gaps
- No reliable France-specific study found on % of spend vs fixed fee for TikTok Ads, or on beginners' realistic monthly revenue in the first year.
- Whether Meta actually shipped "fully automated" ad creation to all advertisers by Oct 2026 was not confirmed. The sources only confirm the plan (2025) and progressive Advantage+ features (2026).
- Google AI Max for Search / PMax details for 2026 were not researched (search returned nothing citable).

## 2. Lead-generation agencies / pay-per-lead / rank-and-rent / lead-gen sites sold to artisans and professions (France): pricing and legality

### Takeaway
Selling leads to artisans is an established, legal market in France if the leads are inbound (the consumer fills in a form). Platforms charge about €10–€180 per shared lead, and exclusive energy-renovation leads cost about €26–€95. However, 2025–2026 law has hit the hottest vertical hard. Since 1 July 2025, any unsolicited solicitation (phone, SMS, email, social networks) for energy-saving or renewable-energy works is banned. Since 11 Aug 2026, all phone prospecting of consumers is opt-in. The beneficiary of illegal calls is presumed liable and the contract is void. On top of that, MaPrimeRénov' budget cuts in 2026 (by-geste route largely cut from 1 Sept 2026, €351M removed in Oct 2026) are shrinking renovation demand. Lead-gen sites (local SEO + Google Ads + forms) remain viable, with RGPD-compliant consent for passing leads to partners. Dentists and lawyers are possible, but under strict professional advertising rules.

### Cited Findings
**Prices per lead (France, 2026)**
- HabitatPresto (an SEO/affiliate-style blog, not HabitatPresto itself): artisans pay €10–€60 HT per contact by job type, or subscriptions of about €150–€400 HT/month. Artisans "pay as soon as they receive the contact", and conversion is "1 in 3 to 1 in 5" (unverified) — [habitat86](https://www.habitat86.com/quels-sont-les-tarifs-d-habitatpresto/)
- A conflicting estimate puts HabitatPresto at €25–50 per lead for painting, €60–120 for boilers and €80–180 for solar panels. It cites 3–5 artisans per lead and 8–14% transformation. Travaux.com is €30–70 HT per lead, and HelloPro has €100/€200/€300 subscriptions plus €25–80 per extra lead (May 2026) — [CalculateurBTP](https://calculateurbtp.com/comparatifs/habitatpresto-vs-hellopro-vs-travaux-com/)
- HabitatPresto itself says the monthly subscription price is custom by trade and département — [Habitatpresto Pro](https://www.habitatpresto.com/pro/conseils/pratiques/habitatpresto-mise-en-relation-travaux)
- Travaux.com charges €1–90 per lead shared with 5–10 competitors. An exclusive lead is "worth 5–10×" a lead shared with 10 artisans. Unvalidated leads contain "30–50% curiosités" — [Fondara](https://www.fondara.fr/blog/plateformes-artisan-batiment-comparatif)
- Energy-renovation lead prices (vendor: Sunleads, 2026), shared / exclusive:

  | Job type | Shared lead | Exclusive lead |
  |---|---|---|
  | Heat pump | €20–40 | €40–80 |
  | Solar panels | €15–30 | €35–65 |
  | Insulation | €12–25 | €30–55 |
  | Global renovation | €25–45 | €50–95 |

  Sunleads' own exclusive leads start at €26 — [Sunleads](https://sunleads.fr/comment-generer-des-leads-renovation-energetique/)
- ONE ONLINE sells dental leads with a Doctolib appointment, quoted on request, minimum first order of 50 leads. It notes "a booked appointment guarantees neither attendance nor treatment" — [ONE ONLINE](https://www.one-online.fr/blog/cout-par-lead-btoc-france)
- Tools such as kelvin° (simulators on artisans' sites) claim one renovation company went from 2–3 quote requests a month to 605 leads. This is a vendor testimonial — [kelvin°](https://www.go-kelvin.com/partenaires/synerciel)

**Legality: phone and solicitation**
- Code de la consommation art. L223-1 (in force 11 Aug 2026, loi n°2025-594 of 30 June 2025):
  - Phone prospecting of a consumer, directly or via a third party, is banned without prior consent. The professional must prove that consent.
  - Phone prospecting of consumers for works or equipment for energy savings, renewable energy or adaptation to ageing or handicap is banned outright, except within an ongoing contract.
  - A professional who "a tiré profit" of illegal calls is presumed responsible.
  - Any contract concluded after illegal phone prospecting is void.

  — [Légifrance L223-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051830285/2026-08-11)
- Décret n°2026-662 of 23 July 2026: the consent request must state the identity of the professional and any third party and the nature of the goods. Consent lasts at most 1 year, is never tacitly renewed, and can be withdrawn orally — [Légifrance R223](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032807254)
- DGCCRF: Bloctel ends with the opt-in regime. Consent can be collected "lors d'un achat, d'une visite en magasin ou via un formulaire", with traceability — [DGCCRF fiche, 27/07/2026](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/les-fiches-pratiques/les-regles-du-demarchage-telephonique-simposant-aux-entreprises)
- A lawyer's blog says that since 1 July 2025 unsolicited solicitation by phone, SMS, email or social networks is banned for energy renovation and housing adaptation works (secondary source; the broader channel scope should be verified in the primary text) — [Kohen Avocats](https://kohenavocats.fr/2026/07/01/demarchage-renovation-energetique-interdit-devis-signe-recours)
- CNIL: transmitting data to partners for electronic prospecting requires consent. The CNIL issued 10 sanctions on prospecting in its 2025 report — [CNIL bilan 2025](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/bilan-sanctions-2025); [CNIL transmission partenaires](https://www.cnil.fr/sites/cnil/files/atoms/files/transmission_des_donnees_a_des_partenaires.pdf)
- CNIL page on reusing publicly accessible data: it remains personal data subject to RGPD — [CNIL prospection](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/la-prospection-commerciale)

**Regulated professions**
- Lawyers (Décret 2023-552, art. 15): advertising and "sollicitation personnalisée" are allowed if sincere, with no comparative or denigrating elements. Personalised solicitation must be by post or email, and SMS is excluded. The Paris RIB (internal rules of the Paris bar) also excludes physical or phone approaches and forbids using a third party to circumvent these rules. Advertising must be sent to the Ordre — [Légifrance art. 15](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000047776450/2026-09-18); [Barreau de Paris RIBP 2026](https://codedeonto.avocatparis.org/media/com_deonto/ribps/ribp_60/RIBP.pdf); [CNB vade-mecum 2020](https://cnb.avocat.fr/medias/cnb-vademecum-communication-des-avocats-2020-68f777f2d9f7f2.25585738.pdf)
- CNB: a third-party "prescripteur" must itself respect the rules imposed on lawyers — [CNB vade-mecum](https://cnb.avocat.fr/medias/cnb-vademecum-communication-des-avocats-2020-68f777f2d9f7f2.25585738.pdf)

**Market demand shock in renovation (2026)**
- The MaPrimeRénov' window was suspended from 1 Jan 2026 and reopened on 23 Feb 2026. Full renovations are limited to homes rated E/F/G, and an appointment with a France Rénov' advisor is now mandatory. The initial 2026 budget was €3.6bn — [ecologie.gouv.fr](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/actualites/reouverture-du-guichet-maprimerenov); [info.gouv.fr](https://www.info.gouv.fr/actualite/reouverture-de-maprimerenov)
- On 14 Oct 2026 the Anah (housing agency) cut €351M from MaPrimeRénov' 2026. The by-geste route lost 40,000 files and was refocused on heat pumps, with funding moved to CEE. New full-renovation applications were reportedly "divided by four" after earlier cuts — [Sud Ouest/AFP](https://www.sudouest.fr/economie/maprimerenov-le-budget-d-aide-a-la-renovation-energetique-officiellement-ampute-de-350-millions-d-euros-31075634.php)
- CAPEB president (31 Aug 2026): energy renovation activity has been falling for about 4 years, with around 40,000 jobs lost in firms under 10 employees over 2 years. The by-geste route was "largely cut" from 1 Sept 2026 — [DNA](https://www.dna.fr/economie/2026/08/31/avec-les-nouvelles-regles-de-maprimerenov-les-artisans-du-batiment-craignent-une-baisse-d-activite)

**Reality checks on lead-gen agencies**
- On Reddit's r/LeadGeneration, an agency owner reported 90% client churn, which they attributed to taking "day-1 clients" with no product-market fit. The source is a vendor blog summarising Reddit — [Linkeddit](https://linkeddit.com/blog/reddit-lead-gen-mistakes-lessons)

### Inferences
- **Rank-and-rent** means building a local site ("plombier-[ville].fr"), ranking it, then renting it or selling its leads to one artisan. It is legal in France if the leads are inbound and the form clearly names who will contact the person (partner-transmission consent). The founder already builds websites, so it fits their skills well. The risks are slow SEO, the AI Overviews launch in France (section 4) cutting organic clicks, and the Google Business Profile being the real driver of local leads.
- Avoid energy-renovation lead resale that relies on outbound calling, SMS or social DMs. The "presumed responsible" clause shifts liability to the buyer, so serious artisans will demand proof of consent. Inbound heat-pump leads remain legal, but demand is falling with the subsidy cuts.
- Pay-per-lead pricing: shared leads at €15–60 are commoditised by HabitatPresto and Travaux.com. A solo operator can only compete on exclusive, local, verified leads (€40–100) or a monthly "site + ads + leads" retainer.
- Safer verticals than energy renovation: urgent home services (plumbing, locksmith, roofing repairs), where calls are inbound. Beware that the locksmith/dépannage niche is notorious for scams and consumer complaints, so reputational risk applies.

### Gaps
- No primary-text confirmation found of the exact channels (SMS, email, social) covered by the July 2025 energy-renovation solicitation ban. It needs a lawyer or a check of loi 2025-594 art. 13.
- No reliable French data found on rank-and-rent rental prices or on solo operators' revenue.
- No primary source found for current dentist advertising rules (Code de la santé publique, R.4127-215 as amended 2020). Only a Canadian page surfaced.
- It is unclear whether a callback to a consumer who submitted a web form counts as "prospection" needing the new phone consent. DGCCRF suggests a form can collect consent, but the wording must meet the décret's requirements.

## 3. Appointment setting / outsourced SDR / cold-email agencies (Instantly, Lemlist, La Growth Machine, Clay): pricing and French legal context

### Takeaway
B2B appointment setting is legal and active in France. B2B email and phone prospecting stays on an opt-out (legitimate interest) basis, while B2C phone moved to opt-in on 11 Aug 2026. French market prices are about €220 per qualified B2B meeting on average, €350–€1,200 in IT, or €2,800–€9,000/month retainers. Tooling is cheap (lemlist from about $55–109/user/month). It is a sales-heavy, deliverability-heavy job with high churn, because clients often lack product-market fit. A good fit for a non-technical person who likes writing and selling, if they niche down (e.g., selling to associations' suppliers, or B2B services).

### Cited Findings
- B2B rule: the opt-in requirement concerns only calls to consumers. Between professionals the CNIL uses legitimate interest (opt-out): the call must relate to the person's activity, use professional contact details, clearly identify the caller and offer immediate opposition (vendor guide, Sept 2026) — [Initiative CRM](https://www.initiative-crm.com/guide/prospection-vente/demarchage-telephonique-b2b)
- CNIL: electronic prospecting requires prior consent for individuals, while professionals must be able to object — [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/la-prospection-commerciale)
- CNIL hosted a webinar on 19 Oct 2026 on the new phone-prospecting consent regime — [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/la-prospection-commerciale)
- Pricing (NewDeal, a French lead-store vendor, May 2026):
  - The average French B2B meeting cost "around €220" in 2025 for a classic decision-maker.
  - IT/DSI meetings cost €350–500 at SMEs and €700–1,200 at large accounts.
  - By channel: outbound phone €350–650, LinkedIn €280–480, cold email €220–380.
  - Models: retainer €4,500–9,000/month for 12–20 meetings; pay-per-meeting €450–900; revenue share 8–15% of the contract over 12 months.

  It claims the new law will raise phone meeting costs by 8–15% and mentions a ban on calling personal mobiles and 36 months of consent traceability. This conflicts with the opt-out B2B reading above and is unverified — [NewDeal](https://www.newdealtheleadstore.com/articles/prix-rendez-vous-b2b-qualifie)
- US/global benchmarks (2026): retainers of $3,000–10,000+/month. Per-meeting prices are $50–300 for SMB and $300–600 for mid-market — [Leadium](https://www.leadium.com/blog/appointment-setting-services). A wider range of $30–1,700 per meeting across segments — [Virtustant](https://www.virtustant.com/blog/appointment-setting-services)
- Tool costs:
  - lemlist Email plan: $69/month ($55 billed yearly), 50k emails/month. Multichannel plan (LinkedIn, SMS, dialer): $109/month ($87 yearly). Includes a 650M+ lead database and AI agents ("lemAgent") — [lemlist pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)
  - lemwarm warm-up: $24–40 per mailbox per month — [lemwarm](https://www.lemwarm.com/fr/pricing)
  - Reported 2026 prices for La Growth Machine: $69/user Email Pro, $99 Multi-Channel Expert (via lemlist's own comparison) — [lemlist blog](https://www.lemlist.com/fr/blog/lemlist-vs-smartlead)
- Reality check: a Reddit-sourced post describes a client paying $2,500/month for "750 cold calls per week, 150 emails per day" yielding 2–4 meetings. Agencies see about 90% churn when clients lack product-market fit. Community consensus is "$100–250 per qualified meeting". Source is a vendor promoting a DIY Reddit tool — [Linkeddit](https://linkeddit.com/blog/lead-gen-agency-churn-diy-alternative)
- Agencies' own pipelines are weak too. SparkToro's surveys show 66% of agencies get clients mainly by referral, only about 6% via outbound, and only 14% call their pipeline healthy — [Enrich Labs citing SparkToro](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/agency-lead-generation-complete-guide-2026)

### Inferences
- For a French solo founder, the realistic offer is "outbound as a service" for B2B SMEs. Pricing would be a setup fee (domains, warm-up, list via Clay or lemlist) plus €1,000–2,500/month, or pay-per-meeting at €150–300 for SMB targets.
- B2C phone setting is now legally hazardous (consent proof, 1-year validity, presumed liability). Stay B2B.
- AI shifts the work to signal-based lists and personalisation at scale. Deliverability (domains, warm-up, volume caps) is the main technical skill. It is learnable, but it is the key failure point.

### Gaps
- No Malt barometer figure found specifically for freelance SDRs or appointment setters. One Malt listing shows a B2B prospecting freelancer at €130/day, which suggests a low-price market.
- Gmail and Microsoft bulk-sender rules (2024–2025) affecting cold email were not re-verified for 2026.
- No French practitioner revenue data found (e.g., LinkedIn posts by La Growth Machine agency founders).

## 4. Local SEO, Google Business Profile management, review/reputation management, and GEO/AEO (being cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews / AI Mode)

### Takeaway
This is the best fit for the founder: it extends web design, is recurring, needs little technical skill and AI does much of the production work. Google Business Profile (GBP) management sells for about €79–€700/month in France, with a credible solo-consultant band of €150–500/month. Google launched AI Overviews and AI Mode in France on 22 July 2026, creating demand for "GEO" (visibility in AI answers). French GEO projects have a median of about €5–7k, and published monthly retainers have an entry median of about $3,250. The market is young, measurement is immature and snake-oil is common. Fake reviews are a DGCCRF enforcement priority, so reputation work must stay clean.

### Cited Findings
**GBP / local SEO pricing (France 2026)**
- Low-cost offers: Web Ranker charges €89 HT/month plus €149 setup (launch offer €79 HT/month) — [Web Ranker](https://webranker.fr/referencement-local-seo). Business Profile Agency charges €109/month (list price €139) or €998/year — [Business Profile Agency](https://go.businessprofile.agency/pricing)
- Freelance GBP consultant: €150–300/month basic, €300–500 complete, €200–400 for an audit. It warns that under €100/month a serious follow-up is impossible — [Boustacom](https://boustacom.fr/blog/consultant-google-business-profile-freelance-2026)
- Full monthly GBP management: €400–800/month (same author, a different article, a slightly inconsistent range) — [Boustacom](https://boustacom.fr/blog/tarif-gestion-fiche-google-2026)
- Local SEO for artisans: €300–600 for one-off GBP optimisation, €600–1,500 for an optimised 5–10 page site, €200–500/month for follow-up. "80% of local results come from the GBP, reviews and city/service pages". Freelance SEO day rate is €400–800 (average about €570) — [OF Coaching](https://of-coaching.fr/guide/tarif-seo-2026)
- Tools: BrightLocal from $39/month (agency plan $199/month). Podium from $399/month — [The Stacc](https://thestacc.com/fr/blog/google-business-profile-tools)

**GEO / AEO**
- Google launched AI Overviews ("Aperçus IA") and AI Mode in France on 22 July 2026. France was the last major Western European market. It also launched a Search Console control in France to opt a site out of generative features — [Real Internet Sales quoting Google](https://www.realinternetsales.com/google-ai-overviews-ai-mode-france-geo-playbook/); [BDM](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/google-deploie-ai-overviews-ai-mode-france/); [Le Monde](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/pixels/article/2026/07/22/google-s-ai-overview-now-available-in-france-raises-anticipation-and-concern_6755715_13.html)
- Click-loss evidence where AI Overviews appear (secondary compilation): −34.5% outbound organic clicks in a randomised field experiment (Jan–Feb 2026), and German position-1 CTR falling from 27% to 11% — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/google-ai-mode-france-launch-eu-publishers-seo-2026)
- Pichai (Q2 2026): AI Mode has passed 1 billion monthly users since its global expansion — [Real Internet Sales quoting Google](https://www.realinternetsales.com/google-ai-overviews-ai-mode-france-geo-playbook/)
- French GEO pricing (La Fabrique du Net, 249 agency budgets, calculated 16 Oct 2026): median €7,000, mean €14,567. 37% of projects are under €5k (diagnostic) and 22% are €10–25k (GEO+SEO strategy). It also states a median of €5,000, an internal inconsistency — [La Fabrique du Net](https://www.lafabriquedunet.fr/agences/pages/agences-geo/tarifs)
- GET-GEO survey of 60 GEO agencies cited by AI (Sept 2026): 47% publish prices, the median entry retainer is $3,250/month and the middle half is $1,950–4,681. Its own pricing is an audit at $750–1,500, a 90-day pilot at $2,500–6,000 and $1,500–3,000/month ongoing. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit costs $99/month per domain for 25 tracked prompts — [GET-GEO](https://get-geo.ai/fr/guides/geo-pricing)
- A French GEO/SEO freelancer is listed on Malt at €360/day — [Malt LinkedIn tag](https://www.malt.fr/s/tags/linkedin-59ca868b6e6c295df40aa411)

**Reviews / reputation: legal limits**
- DGCCRF flags biased moderation (deleting or delaying negative reviews) and fake reviews, and uses the "Polygraphe" tool to detect them — [DGCCRF fiche](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/les-fiches-pratiques/avis-en-ligne-attention-aux-faux-commentaires); [DGCCRF enquête](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/actualites-dgccrf/comment-la-dgccrf-enquete-sur-les-faux-avis)

### Inferences
- Natural bundle for the founder's existing clients and adjacent SMBs: "site + GBP + review collection (QR codes, SMS/email requests to actual customers) + AI-drafted review replies + monthly report" at €100–300/month. It is very low-skill, AI writes the posts and replies, and it is recurring.
- GEO as an add-on: an "AI visibility audit" (testing prompts in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode, then fixing structured data, FAQ content, citations and directories) at €300–1,500 one-off. Sell it to associations as well, since donors and volunteers also ask ChatGPT. The timing (France launch July 2026) creates a short-lived awareness window. Avoid promising citations, which no one can guarantee.
- Saturation: GBP management at €79–109/month is already commoditised. Differentiate by vertical (e.g., associations, health professionals, artisans in one département).

### Gaps
- No independent French study of AI Overviews' click impact since the July 2026 launch (too recent).
- No reliable data on how often local queries (e.g., "plombier Nantes") trigger AI Overviews in France.

## 7. Growth / lead-gen for specific verticals hot in France in 2026, plus cross-cutting realism

### Takeaway
2026 has reshuffled French verticals. Energy renovation (heat pumps, insulation, solar) was the historical cash cow of French lead-gen, but it is now legally fenced off (solicitation ban, opt-in, presumed liability) and shrinking with subsidy cuts. AI search's arrival (July 2026) creates demand for local SEO, GBP and GEO. B2B outbound remains legal (opt-out). Regulated professions (lawyers, dentists) are reachable via compliant advertising and appointment-booking funnels. Every model faces high churn and inflated income claims. Referrals dominate how agencies themselves win clients.

### Cited Findings
- Energy renovation: the phone-prospecting ban, opt-in, and void contracts are in force 11 Aug 2026 — [Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051830285/2026-08-11). The MaPrimeRénov' cut of €351M is refocusing on heat pumps — [Sud Ouest](https://www.sudouest.fr/economie/maprimerenov-le-budget-d-aide-a-la-renovation-energetique-officiellement-ampute-de-350-millions-d-euros-31075634.php). Heat pumps, geothermal heat pumps, heat-network connection, oil-tank removal and energy audits remain eligible by-geste — [Generali](https://www.generali.fr/actu/financement-travaux-renovation)
- Rental regulation: G-rated homes have been banned from rental since 1 Jan 2025, F-rated from 2028 and E-rated from 2034. A 2026 DPE (energy rating) reform lets about 850,000 homes exit "passoire" status without works — [Generali](https://www.generali.fr/actu/financement-travaux-renovation)
- Dental: a French lead seller offers dental leads with systematic Doctolib appointments — [ONE ONLINE](https://www.one-online.fr/blog/cout-par-lead-btoc-france)
- Lawyers: advertising and email/post solicitation are allowed, but SMS and phone are not — [Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000047776450/2026-09-18)
- AI search in France since July 2026 — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/google-ai-mode-france-launch-eu-publishers-seo-2026)
- Agency client acquisition: 66% of agencies cite existing or past clients as their top referral source and 80.5% believe a vertical niche helps acquisition. SparkToro 2025: 50% of agencies grew, 23% shrank, and 41% say it is "a struggle" — [Enrich Labs citing SparkToro/Databox](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/agency-lead-generation-complete-guide-2026)
- AI-agency vs SMMA claims: AI receptionist/automation agencies tout 70–96% margins and "low competition". These are vendor claims from companies selling white-label platforms or courses (e.g., Ciela "Client Accelerator $1,499"), and Reddit notes the marketing side is "flooded with course sellers" — [Ciela AI](https://ciela.ai/blogs/is-starting-an-ai-automation-agency-worth-it-reddit); [VoiceAI Connect](https://www.myvoiceaiconnect.com/blog/ai-receptionist-agency-vs-smma)
- The CNIL issued 16 simplified-procedure sanctions since May 2025 (€108k in total) and 10 prospecting-related sanctions in its 2025 report — [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/la-cnil-prononce-16-nouvelles-sanctions-dans-le-cadre-de-la-procedure-simplifiee); [CNIL bilan 2025](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/bilan-sanctions-2025)

### Inferences
Ranking of business models for this specific founder (judgement based on the findings above):

| Rank | Model | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Local SEO + GBP + reviews + "AI visibility" (GEO) retainer | Extends web design; recurring €100–500/month; AI does production; timely in France |
| 2 | Lead-gen / rank-and-rent sites for inbound local services (not energy renovation, not outbound) | Uses web skills; legal with inbound forms and consent; slow to build |
| 3 | LinkedIn ghostwriting for ESS / association / foundation leaders | €600–1,500/month; writing plus AI; leverages the founder's network |
| 4 | Brevo email automation for associations, possibly expanding to SMB e-commerce Klaviyo | Same flows discipline, existing market first |
| 5 | B2B appointment setting / cold email | Legal (opt-out) but sales- and deliverability-heavy; high churn |
| 6 | Paid ads management | Most commoditised by Meta/Google AI; only worth it bundled with landing pages |

- Realistic revenue (inference, unverified): with retainers of €150–500/month, reaching €3–5k MRR (monthly recurring revenue) requires about 10–25 clients. That is consistent with the vendor framing that SMMA needs "3–10 clients at $500–3,000" for $5k profit, but at French SMB price points.
- Avoid any model built on B2C cold calling, SMS blasts or buying "fichiers" (contact lists) without RGPD-compliant consent chains.
