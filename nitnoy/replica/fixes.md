# Fixes and angle (replica-entrepreneur, scaled down)

No review corpus was collected for this build: the clone is for one learner,
Kevin, and the brief itself is the "what users hate" list. No quotes, no
counts, no invented reviews. Everything below comes from Kevin's own words
in the brief, so the sample size is one.

## What to fix, from the brief

1. "Pas un million de machins": one tight vocabulary (about 100 words, 50 phrases) that covers the first conversations in Chiang Mai. No 40-language catalogue, no grammar theory.
2. "Un truc où je parle": every lesson ends with speaking steps, and every unit ends with a roleplay you must say line by line. In Duolingo, speaking is optional and rare; in Speak, it is the whole app. Nitnoy puts Speak's loop inside Duolingo's path.
3. "Progresser vite": no hearts, no ads, no waiting. Wrong answers come back at the end of the same lesson. A lesson is 12-18 steps, about 3 minutes.
4. "Me débrouiller": a phrasebook with search and audio, usable at a market stall with one hand.
5. "Pratiquer mon anglais aussi": the interface and every meaning are in English. No French anywhere in the app.
6. Thai specific: the polite particle and the pronoun change with the speaker's gender; a switch handles it everywhere.

## Angle

A little Thai every day, said out loud. One file, no account, works offline.
